Heavy Utility Platform: Off-Road Robot Mower

Project Class: Open-Source Hardware / Industrial Automation
Core Concept: Salvaging a heavy-duty electric wheelchair chassis and replacing the standard joystick brain with high-amperage motor controllers and a hobby RC interface for safe, remote utility tracking.

📊 System Specifications

Component Class Hardware Choice / Type Purpose / Technical Target
Chassis & Drive Electric Wheelchair Frame High torque differential steering, robust terrain handling
Motor Driver Sabertooth Dual 60A Motor Driver Handling immense current spikes and regenerative braking safely
Control Link Standard ExpressLRS (ELRS) TX/RX Ultra-reliable, long-range fail-safe radio frequency transmission
Power Bus Dual 12V Batteries (24V Native) Direct raw high-capacity power distribution to drivetrain
⚠️ High Torque / High Voltage Safety Notice: Wheelchair motors carry immense rotational torque capable of causing structural or physical damage. Always perform initial drivetrain bench tests with the drive wheels completely elevated off the ground.

🛠️ Build Log & Assembly Instructions

Phase 1: Chassis Teardown & Safe Electrical Isolation

The first structural phase required stripping away all user-focused commercial elements of the source wheelchair to reduce overall profile scale and establish a safe workspace environment.

  • Unbolted and completely removed the main heavy seating rig.
  • Gained direct line-of-sight access to the lower base chassis housing the factory electrical system, primary fuse links, and battery compartment.
  • Safety Protocol: Immediately disconnected and removed the heavy lead-acid batteries to fully de-energise the chassis before modifying any stock electronics or factory loom configurations.

Phase 2: OEM Electronics Stripping & Motor Pinout Analysis

With the platform isolated safely, the entire stock proprietary wiring harness and commercial joystick processing units were entirely stripped out of the core compartment tray.

  • Removed all factory motor controllers and proprietary breakout boxes, leaving only the clean, raw battery terminal leads and direct motor lines.
  • Technical Reverse-Engineering: Isolated and identified the 4-wire pinout coming from each independent geared drive assembly:
    • 2x Large High-Gauge Wires: The main continuous DC current delivery lines to supply voltage and control rotational drive parameters.
    • 2x Small Low-Gauge Wires: Dedicated electromagnetic parking brake terminals. These require a constant auxiliary 24V supply to fully disengage the mechanical friction lock rings and allow free motor armature rotation.

Phase 3: Custom Thermal Mount & Drivetrain Integration

To safely mount the new control hardware, a custom mechanical mounting solution was fabricated from raw metal stock to handle the high-current demands of the Sabertooth 2x60A driver.

  • Heatsink Fabrication: Measured, cut, and drilled a custom structural base plate out of thick aluminium stock. This plate serves a critical dual purpose: securely anchoring the speed controller to the electronics bay while acting as a heavy-duty passive thermal heatsink to conduct operational heat away from the driver's FETs.
  • Route the main high-gauge motor power leads straight into the Sabertooth motor output screw terminals.
  • Brake Bypass Logic: Because the Sabertooth features powerful integrated regenerative electronic braking, the mechanical parking brakes are redundant. The two smaller brake wires from each motor were safely wired into a constant 24V supply loop to ensure the friction locks remain completely disengaged whenever the machine is powered on.

Phase 4: Signal Control Link & Fail-Safe Calibration

The final phase required establishing the radio control link using the ExpressLRS protocol and calibrating the system for maximum operational safety.

  • PWM Signal Routing: Connected the ExpressLRS receiver outputs directly to the Sabertooth signal input terminals, setting the driver dip switches to microcontroller/RC mode.
  • Mixing Configurations: Configured the radio transmitter with custom elevon/v-tail mixing parameters to enable intuitive single-stick differential steering control.
  • Fail-Safe Protocol: Programmed the receiver to instantly cut all PWM signal pulses if the radio frequency connection drops, engaging the Sabertooth's automatic emergency stop sequence.
