KRL National Championships 2026

Event Status: Upcoming / Under Active Preparation

Tactical Strategy & Goals

The upcoming National Championships represent the ultimate validation test for our latest hardware builds. Following our October first-place victory, the engineering focus shifts from basic survival to scaling durability under continuous tournament wear-and-tear.

Our core goal is to maintain high-mobility dominance while safeguarding our modular weapon assemblies against heavy vertical spinners.

Pre-Event Readiness Checklist

  • [ ] Finalize Modular Armor Configs: Machine backup sets of TPU shock mounts and 4mm titanium top plates.
  • [ ] Stress-Test Drive Subsystems: Execute a full 3-minute continuous stress cycle to guarantee no thermal degradation or current spiking occurs.
  • [ ] Inspect Weapon Bearings: Press-fit fresh ceramic bearings into the primary kinetic spinner assembly to optimize spin-up runtime.
  • [ ] Battery Array Pre-Cycling: Cycle and map internal resistance profiles for all 4S LiPo flight packs to ensure peak voltage delivery during elimination rounds.
