Engineering & Robotics Projects

3lb Combat Robot: Baba Yaga (v3 / v4)

Status: v4 in Progress

Baba Yaga is a 3lb combat robot built for competitive events. Version 3 successfully proved out the electronics and basic weapon layout. I am currently manufacturing and documenting Version 4 to address drive durability and upgrade the weapon assembly.

Off-Road Robot Mower

Status: Open-Source Documentation Phase

An open-source heavy utility project converting an old electric wheelchair chassis into a high-clearance, off-road remote/autonomous lawnmower. I am hosting full build logs, schematic wiring diagrams, and assembly instructions here for others to copy.

30lb Combat Robot Design

Status: CAD & Conceptual Design

Stepping up to the featherweight class with a structural overhaul. This design translates the spin and weapon architecture of Baba Yaga into a robust, heavy-duty welded steel frame engineered to withstand massive kinetic impacts.
