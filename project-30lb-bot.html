30lb Combat Robot Design

The Steel Evolution

This project takes the weapon geometry and physical architecture validated on Baba Yaga and scales it up to a 30lb featherweight class frame.

Engineering Targets

  • Chassis: Heavy welded structural steel assembly designed to absorb immense direct kinetic forces.
  • Power Delivery: Upgraded electronic speed controller setups targeting high amperage spikes without thermalling out.
