KRL Fight Day October 2026

🏆 1st Place Winner
Event Date: October 2026

Executive Summary

An incredible tournament milestone. After identifying fatal drive system and electrical vulnerabilities during July's debut, Baba Yaga underwent a complete internal overhaul. The structural and electronic upgrades paid off flawlessly. Baba Yaga went the distance, adapting perfectly to changing cage conditions, and securing a hard-fought overall first-place victory.

Match Log & Tournament Run

  • Round 1 vs. Shredder: Won via KO (1:12). Internal shock mounting absorbed high-energy kinetic impacts effortlessly. A clean weapon hit disabled Shredder's drive.
  • Quarter-Finals vs. Cobalt-X: Won via Unanimous Decision. A tactical pushing match. The newly implemented high-traction wheels gave Baba Yaga total control over the arena hazard zones.
  • Semi-Finals vs. Overload: Won via KO (2:05). Overload's vertical spinner deflected off our hardened titanium wedge, allowing us to counter-attack and breach their side armor.
  • Grand Finals vs. Apex: Won via Split Decision. A brutal 3-minute endurance war. Apex shattered part of our outer top plate, but Baba Yaga's modular internal layout kept the receiver and ESCs alive to secure the win on aggression points.

Engineering Insights & Iterations

What Worked:

  • Reinforced Drive System: Upgraded brushless motors with custom planetary gearboxes provided bulletproof reliability under heavy load.
  • Electrical Isolation: Opto-isolated signal lines completely eradicated the power surge brownouts experienced in July.
  • Cage Adaptability: The modular wedge configurations allowed fast swaps between a low-ground clearance scraper and a heavy impact guard.

Future Refinements for Nationals:

  • Top Armor Upgrades: Grand finals exposed structural flexing in the 1.5mm top plate. This needs upgrading to 2.5mm carbon fibre or AR400 steel.
  • Weapon Spin-up Optimization: Lower the gear ratio slightly to reduce current spikes during weapon acceleration.
