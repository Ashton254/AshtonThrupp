KRL Fight Day July 2026

Event Date: July 2026 | Status: Competitive Debut

Executive Summary & Robot Specs

This event marked the highly anticipated competitive combat debut for the latest iteration of Baba Yaga. Going into the arena, our focus was testing the integration of the new custom armor layout and weapon mechanics. While the build showed offensive promise, the tournament served as an aggressive testing ground that exposed critical flaws in our powertrain.

Critical Breakdowns & Limit Testing

1. Drive System Thermal Limits

Within the first 45 seconds of heavy maneuvering during Match 1, the drive motors experienced extreme heat dissipation issues. The lack of active ventilation or specialized heat-sinking within the compact chassis caused the motors to rapidly hit thermal throttling limits, severely undercutting our agility.

2. Electrical Configuration Vulnerabilities

During a high-impact collision in Match 2, a sudden voltage sag from the main battery link caused a transient reset on the internal microcontrollers. This brief loss of signal loop left the robot unresponsive for three crucial seconds, leaving us exposed to incoming kinetic strikes.

Lessons Learnt & Action Items

  • Overhaul Powertrain Thermals: Redesign motor mounts to act as structural heat sinks and integrate airflow channels into the baseplate.
  • Isolate Electronics Logics: Add a dedicated opto-isolated backup regulator for the control board to prevent full system resets during peak battery current draws.
  • Secure Connectors: Replace friction-fit links with positive-locking connectors to better survive severe mechanical shock.
